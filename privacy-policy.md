## 简体中文

### 一、我们如何处理你的图片

好标记是一款**在设备本地完成图片标注**的工具。你导入的图片、绘制的标注、裁剪与马赛克等操作**全部在你的设备上完成，不会上传到我们或任何第三方服务器**。我们没有账号系统，也不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱等身份信息。

- **导入图片**：通过系统相册选择器、相机，或你手动输入的图片网址。
- **输入图片网址时**：App 会直接从该网址所在的服务器下载图片，请确认你信任该来源。
- **导出与分享**：导出的图片保存在你的相册中，或通过系统分享面板由你自行选择接收方。

### 二、本地存储的数据

为了让你下次能继续编辑，App 会在你的设备本地保存：

- **最近编辑**：最近一次编辑的图片与标注（用于首页「最近编辑」入口）。
- **收藏**：你主动收藏的图片与标注。
- **设置**：界面语言选择。

以上数据均保存在设备本地（iOS 使用应用沙盒内的数据库文件），我们不接触、不备份、不上传。**卸载 App 即会随之删除**；你也可以在 App 内删除单条收藏。

### 三、广告与第三方 SDK

App 使用 **Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）** 在首页底部展示一条横幅广告，以支持应用的持续开发。为此，Google 及其广告合作伙伴可能收集和处理以下信息：

- 设备标识符（如允许追踪时的广告标识符 IDFA；不允许时为非个性化广告）
- 广告数据（广告展示、点击等互动信息）
- 大致位置（由 IP 地址推导的国家/地区级别）
- 诊断与防欺诈所需的设备与网络信息

这些信息的处理适用 [Google 隐私权政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy) 与 [Google 广告技术说明](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)。我们自身**不会**读取或存储上述广告数据。

### 四、你的选择与同意

- **iOS 追踪授权（ATT）**：首次使用广告功能时，系统会询问是否允许追踪。**拒绝后你仍可正常使用全部功能**，只是会看到非个性化广告。你可随时在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 追踪」中修改。
- **欧盟/英国等地区（UMP 同意）**：我们会先展示 Google 提供的同意表单；如果你不同意，将不会请求个性化广告。
- **关闭个性化广告**：可在设备的广告设置中限制广告个性化。

### 五、权限用途

| 权限 | 用途 | 可否拒绝 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 照片（读取） | 选择要标注的图片 | 可以（改用拍照或网址导入） |
| 照片（写入） | 将标注后的图片保存到相册 | 可以（改用系统分享） |

### 六、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童（欧盟地区为 16 岁以下），我们不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。若你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会尽快处理。

### 七、信息安全与数据留存

由于图片处理完全在本地进行，我们不存在服务器端的用户数据留存。本地数据的安全依赖于你的设备与系统保护（如锁屏密码、iOS 沙盒机制）。请在分享图片前自行确认其中不含敏感信息。

### 八、政策更新

我们可能会更新本政策以反映功能或法律要求的变化，更新后的版本会发布在本页面并更新「生效日期」。

### 九、联系我们

## English

### 1. How we handle your photos

Good4Mark performs **all image annotation locally on your device**. Photos you import, the annotations you draw, cropping and mosaic blurring are processed entirely on-device and are **never uploaded to us or to any server we control**. There is no account system, and we do not collect your name, phone number, or email address.

- **Importing**: via the system photo picker, the camera, or an image URL you type yourself.
- **When you enter an image URL**, the app downloads the image directly from that server; please only use sources you trust.
- **Export and sharing**: exported images are saved to your photo library, or handed to the system share sheet for you to choose a recipient.

### 2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue editing later, the app stores the following **locally on your device**:

- Your most recent edit (image + annotations) for the home screen's "Recent edits" entry
- Items you explicitly save to Favorites
- Your interface language preference

This data lives in the app sandbox on your device. We cannot access it, and it is not backed up to or transmitted to us. **Uninstalling the app deletes it**, and you can delete individual favorites in-app.

### 3. Advertising and third-party SDK

The app shows one banner ad at the bottom of the home screen using **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)** to support ongoing development. For this purpose, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

- Device identifiers (such as IDFA when tracking is allowed; otherwise non-personalized ads are served)
- Advertising data (impressions, clicks and similar interactions)
- Approximate location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
- Device and network information used for diagnostics and fraud prevention

Such processing is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We ourselves do **not** read or store this advertising data.

### 4. Your choices and consent

- **iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT)**: on first use of ads, iOS asks whether tracking is allowed. **Declining does not limit any app feature** — you will simply see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **EEA/UK (UMP consent)**: we show Google's consent form first; if you do not consent, personalized ads are not requested.
- **Limit ad personalization** in your device's ad settings at any time.

### 5. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Can you decline? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos (read) | Choose an image to annotate | Yes (use camera or URL instead) |
| Photos (write) | Save the annotated image to your library | Yes (use the share sheet instead) |
| Camera | Take a photo to annotate | Yes (use gallery import instead) |
| Tracking (ATT) | Serve more relevant ads | Yes (only affects ad relevance) |

### 6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

### 7. Security and retention

Because image processing happens entirely on-device, we retain no server-side user data. The security of local data depends on your device protections (passcode, iOS sandboxing). Please review images for sensitive content before sharing them.

### 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

### 9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact: **peill@me.com**
